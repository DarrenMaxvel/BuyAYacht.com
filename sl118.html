SL118 Overview: The Sanlorenzo SL118 is a 36.5-meter luxury motor yacht built by Sanlorenzo between 2013 and 2020. Designed by Francesco Paszkowski, the SL118 features a planing hull constructed from GRP, offering a perfect blend of performance and elegance. Accommodating up to 10 guests in 5 en-suite staterooms, including a full-beam master suite on the main deck, she provides spacious and luxurious living areas. The yacht is powered by twin MTU 16V 2000 M94 engines, achieving a top speed of 27 knots and a cruising speed of 20 knots. Notable features include fold-down balconies, expansive flybridge with Jacuzzi, and large floor-to-ceiling windows that flood the interior with natural light.
