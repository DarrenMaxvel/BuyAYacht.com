Aleden Overview: Aleden is a 36.92-meter luxury motor yacht built by Sanlorenzo in 2024, featuring an innovative asymmetric design by Zuccon International Project. This design maximizes internal volume and natural light, offering expansive living spaces and panoramic sea views. Accommodating up to 10 guests in 5 staterooms, including a full-beam master suite on the main deck, Aleden provides unparalleled comfort and elegance. With a cruising speed of 23 knots and a top speed of 27 knots, she combines performance with luxury, making her an exceptional choice for discerning yacht enthusiasts.
