GoldenEye Overview: GoldenEye is a 54-meter Mangusta GranSport 54 motor yacht built by Overmarine Group in 2020. Designed by Alberto Mancini, she combines sleek, aggressive lines with high-performance capabilities. Constructed with an aluminum hull and superstructure, GoldenEye offers a top speed of 29 knots and a cruising speed of 21 knots, powered by four MTU 16V 2000 M96L engines. With a range of 4,200 nautical miles at an economical speed of 12 knots, she is well-suited for both fast cruising and long-range voyages. Accommodating up to 12 guests in 7 luxurious cabins, including a full-beam master suite, and supported by a crew of 9, GoldenEye ensures a comfortable and opulent experience on the water.
